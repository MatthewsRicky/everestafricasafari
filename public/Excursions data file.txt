1. 1 Day Funzi Island Dhow Trip

Itinerary Description
Included / Excluded
Itinerary Route
scape to the unspoiled beauty of Funzi Island on this relaxing full-day dhow cruise through mangrove channels, sandbanks, and pristine beaches. Located south of Diani, this off-the-beaten-path experience offers a perfect blend of coastal scenery, local culture, and laid-back adventure.

Sail in a traditional Swahili dhow, explore the Ramisi River mangroves, visit Funzi village, and unwind on a secluded sandbar—a dream location for swimming, sunbathing, and photography. Enjoy a freshly prepared Swahili seafood lunch at a local island restaurant before cruising back in the afternoon.

Ideal for couples, families, and nature lovers seeking a peaceful day out on the Indian Ocean.

07:00–07:30 AM – Hotel Pick-Up
Pick-up from your hotel or residence in Diani, Ukunda, or Msambweni.
Transfer to Bodo village, the dhow boarding point (approx. 45–60 minutes drive).

Depart on Traditional Dhow
Board a Swahili-style dhow with your guide and crew.
Begin cruising through the calm waters and mangrove channels of the south coast.

Visit Natural Sandbank (Tide Dependent)
Stop at a stunning sandbank in the middle of the ocean for sunbathing, swimming, and photography.
Enjoy breathtaking views and a peaceful break in nature.

Ramisi River Mangrove Tour
Cruise into the Ramisi River estuary, gliding through mangrove forests teeming with birds and marine life.
Learn about the local ecosystem from your guide as you explore these peaceful channels.

Visit to Funzi Island Village
Arrive at Funzi Island and take a short walking tour through the local fishing village.
Meet local residents, learn about Swahili coastal culture, and visit traditional homes or schools (optional).

Swahili Lunch on Funzi Island
Enjoy a freshly prepared Swahili-style seafood lunch at a local island restaurant.
Dishes typically include coconut rice, grilled fish, chapati, seasonal vegetables, and fresh tropical fruit.
Vegetarian options are available on request.

Leisure Cruise Back
After lunch, board the dhow and enjoy a relaxed cruise back to the mainland.

Hotel Drop-Off
Arrive back at Bodo village and transfer to your hotel with great memories of a peaceful, scenic day on Kenya’s south coast.

2. 1 Day Shimba hills

Itinerary Description
Included / Excluded
Itinerary Route
Escape the coastal crowds and venture into the serene wilderness of Shimba Hills National Reserve on this refreshing 1-day safari from Diani or Mombasa’s South Coast. Just a short drive away, Shimba Hills offers a rare blend of tropical forest, rolling hills, and unique wildlife—including the elusive sable antelope, found nowhere else in Kenya.

Enjoy guided game drives, panoramic ocean views, and the option to hike to the scenic Sheldrick Falls, where you can cool off in nature’s tranquility. This day trip is perfect for travelers looking to combine light adventure, nature, and wildlife in a single, enriching experience—without venturing far from the coast.

07:00 AM – Hotel Pick-Up
Pick-up from your hotel in Diani, Ukunda, or nearby coastal areas.
Drive to Shimba Hills National Reserve (approx. 45–60 minutes).

Game Drive Begins
Enter Shimba Hills National Reserve and begin your morning game drive.
Look out for elephants, buffalo, bushbuck, giraffes, and the rare sable antelope—found only here in Kenya.
Enjoy scenic viewpoints like Elephant Hill, overlooking the ocean and forests below.

Visit to Sheldrick Falls (Optional Walk)
Option to hike (about 30–40 minutes each way) to the stunning Sheldrick Falls, accompanied by a local KWS ranger.
Walk through shaded forest paths with possible sightings of birds, butterflies, and monkeys.
Relax by the waterfall and take photos before hiking back to the vehicle.

Lunch Break
Option 1: Enjoy lunch at the Shimba Hills Lodge, a unique tree lodge with views of a waterhole (meals paid directly).
Option 2: Bring a packed picnic lunch to enjoy at a scenic picnic site within the park.

Final Game Drive & Scenic Stop
Short game drive through a different route as you exit the park.
Photo stops at scenic viewpoints such as Marere Dam or Ocean View Point.

Return to Your Hotel
Arrive back at your hotel in Diani or surrounding areas with great photos and memories of a peaceful forest safari.

3. 1 Day Mombasa city Tour

Itinerary Description
Included / Excluded
Itinerary Route
Discover the rich history, vibrant culture, and coastal charm of Kenya’s oldest city on this exciting 1-day Mombasa City Tour. From ancient architecture and bustling markets to stunning sea views and Swahili heritage, this tour takes you through the heart of Mombasa, offering a perfect blend of sightseeing, culture, and local experiences.

Explore the legendary Fort Jesus, stroll through the narrow streets of Old Town, visit colorful spice and curio markets, and take in panoramic views of the Indian Ocean. This day tour is ideal for history lovers, first-time visitors, and anyone curious to see the real Mombasa beyond the beach resorts.

08:00 AM – Hotel Pick-up
Pick-up from your hotel or residence in Mombasa, Nyali, Bamburi, or surrounding areas.
Meet your professional English-speaking guide and begin your city adventure.

Mombasa Old Town Walking Tour
Stroll through the narrow streets of Old Town, rich in Swahili, Arab, and Portuguese influence.
Admire the unique architecture, intricately carved wooden doors, and balconies.
Learn about Mombasa’s early trade history and multicultural heritage.

Fort Jesus Museum
Visit the historic Fort Jesus, built by the Portuguese in 1593 to guard the old port.
Explore the museum exhibits, underground passages, and view the Old Harbor.
Great photo opportunities of the fort walls and sea views.

Spice Market & Curio Shopping
Visit a local spice market to see and learn about traditional coastal spices.
Browse curio shops for handmade crafts, souvenirs, and carvings (optional purchases).

Mombasa Tusks (Photo Stop)
Stop at the iconic Mombasa Tusks on Moi Avenue – built in 1952 to commemorate Queen Elizabeth II’s visit.
Quick photo opportunity and brief history from your guide.

Swahili Lunch (Optional)
Break for lunch at a local Swahili restaurant or café (own expense unless otherwise included).
Try coastal dishes like biryani, pilau, samaki wa kupaka (grilled fish in coconut), and fresh tropical juices.

Mama Ngina Waterfront or Likoni Ferry Viewpoint
Visit the Mama Ngina Waterfront for scenic ocean views, dhow spotting, and relaxed walking paths.
Optional stop at the Likoni Ferry Viewpoint to observe local ferry operations and the busy port area.
Optional Add-Ons (Time Permitting)
Choose one of the following if time allows:
Visit to Haller Park (small entrance fee – nature park with giraffes, crocodiles, tortoises)
Visit to Bombolulu Cultural Centre for local crafts and performances
A short visit to Nyali Beach or public beach market

Drop-off at Hotel
Return transfer to your hotel with memories of a colorful and enriching day in Mombasa.

4. 1 Day Wasini Island Dhow Trip

Itinerary Description
Included / Excluded
Itinerary Route
Sail into paradise with a full-day dhow excursion to Wasini Island, an unforgettable marine adventure along Kenya’s stunning south coast. Departing from Shimoni, this trip takes you through the Kisite-Mpunguti Marine Park, renowned for its crystal-clear waters, vibrant coral reefs, and rich marine life—including dolphins, sea turtles, and tropical fish.

Snorkel in untouched coral gardens, enjoy a traditional Swahili seafood lunch on Wasini Island, and explore the island’s rich culture and serene coastal beauty. Whether you’re a nature lover, snorkeler, or simply seeking a day of relaxation on the Indian Ocean, this dhow trip promises a perfect blend of adventure and tranquility.

06:00 – 07:00 AM: Hotel Pick-up
Pick-up from your hotel or resort in Diani Beach, Ukunda, or surrounding areas.
Drive south through rural villages and coconut plantations to Shimoni, a charming coastal fishing village near the Tanzanian border.

Dhow Departure from Shimoni
Board a traditional Swahili dhow (wooden sailing boat).
Welcome briefing and safety instructions from the crew.
Light refreshments served on board (tea/coffee and biscuits).

Dolphin Spotting & Sail to Marine Park
Cruise through the crystal-clear waters of the Wasini Channel.
Look out for bottlenose and humpback dolphins that frequent this area (sightings are common but not guaranteed).
Proceed toward the Kisite-Mpunguti Marine Park.

Guided Snorkeling Session
Anchor at designated snorkeling spots inside the marine park.
Enjoy 1–1.5 hours of guided snorkeling among vibrant coral reefs and a wide array of colorful fish.
Snorkeling gear (mask, fins, life jacket) is provided.

12:30 PM: Lunch on Wasini Island
Sail to Wasini Island for a freshly prepared Swahili seafood lunch in a local restaurant overlooking the ocean.
Vegetarian options available on request.
Sample dishes may include coconut rice, grilled fish, chapati, vegetables, and tropical fruits.

Optional Cultural Walk (30–45 minutes)
After lunch, you may choose to join a guided village walk to explore:
Traditional Swahili homes and coastal village life
The coral garden (during low tide)
The ancient baobab trees and island history
Alternatively, relax at the restaurant or enjoy ocean views.

Depart Wasini – Return to Shimoni
Board the dhow and sail back to Shimoni jetty.
Transfer to your vehicle for the return journey to Diani.

04:00–05:00 PM: Drop-off at Hotel
Return to your hotel or resort with great memories of your Wasini Island adventure.
