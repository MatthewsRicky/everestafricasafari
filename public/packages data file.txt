08:00 AM – Hotel Pick-up
Pick-up from your hotel or residence in Mombasa, Nyali, Bamburi, or surrounding areas.
Meet your professional English-speaking guide and begin your city adventure.

Mombasa Old Town Walking Tour
Stroll through the narrow streets of Old Town, rich in Swahili, Arab, and Portuguese influence.
Admire the unique architecture, intricately carved wooden doors, and balconies.
Learn about Mombasa’s early trade history and multicultural heritage.

Fort Jesus Museum
Visit the historic Fort Jesus, built by the Portuguese in 1593 to guard the old port.
Explore the museum exhibits, underground passages, and view the Old Harbor.
Great photo opportunities of the fort walls and sea views.

Spice Market & Curio Shopping
Visit a local spice market to see and learn about traditional coastal spices.
Browse curio shops for handmade crafts, souvenirs, and carvings (optional purchases).

Mombasa Tusks (Photo Stop)
